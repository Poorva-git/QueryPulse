Key Capabilities

Everything you need to turn natural questions into business intelligence without writing SQL.

English & Hinglish to SQL

Ask questions naturally like "Top 5 most rented films kaunsi hain?" — the AI understands your schema and writes optimized queries.

AST Security Sandbox

Mathematical syntax tree validation blocks harmful operations (DROP, DELETE, injections) before execution.

Postgres Query Plan & Cost

Runs internal EXPLAIN to calculate query execution cost and primary scan type in real-time.

Auto Visual Charts

Instantly generates interactive Bar, Line, or Doughnut charts. One click to switch views or download raw results as CSV.

Bring Your Own Database (BYODB)

Connect your own Supabase, Neon, or local database in seconds. Credentials stay in your active browser session only.

Executive Business Insights

Every query generates a 2-sentence executive summary explaining what the numbers actually mean for decision-making.
